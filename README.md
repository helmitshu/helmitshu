# Applied AI Engineer

I apply AI to real life problems. AI is a force multiplier, and I refuse to waste it on toy demos. Every repo here ships, and every repo here solves something real.

## What I believe

Most AI work today is decoration. Demos that impress once and help never. I build the opposite. I point AI at problems people actually have, and I ship software that works.

## Featured work

### Sentinel
*Status: live demo and API, validated on real datasets, version 2 in progress.*

Predictive maintenance engine. It watches pump telemetry, learns what normal looks like, and flags failures before they happen, with plain English explanations for every alert.

Validated unsupervised on two public datasets: 98 percent detection rate with a 47.5 cycle median alert lead time on NASA C-MAPSS turbofan data, and AUROC 0.97 on CWRU bearing vibration data.

Live: [dashboard](https://fantastic-miracle-production-65d8.up.railway.app) | [API docs](https://sentinel-production-ab25.up.railway.app/docs)

### VoiceFront
*Status: core platform live and serving calls, security hardening in progress, not consumer ready yet.*

AI voice receptionist platform for clinics, contractors, and service businesses. Answers every call, books appointments straight into the calendar, captures leads, and never misses one. Multi tenant SaaS with a white label dashboard, an admin portal, and 24 live voice assistants in production.

### Mirage
*Status: live and working, iterating.*

See through ghost jobs before you apply. Paste a posting, get a legitimacy score and every red flag in plain English. A rules engine does the first pass, with an optional second opinion from Claude.

Try it live: [mirage-production-db25.up.railway.app](https://mirage-production-db25.up.railway.app)

### LeadLens
*Status: working prototype, runs locally.*

AI sales intelligence. Researches any company worldwide, detects buying signals, and generates personalized outreach in 90 seconds. Cold email, talk track, LinkedIn messages, battle cards, live objection handling, PDF export.
